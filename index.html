01ABOUT ME

RESEARCH MISSION

From isolated models to collaborative industrial intelligence.

My research centers on industrial foundation models and multi-agent systems: learning generalizable representations from heterogeneous shop-floor data, connecting perception with reasoning, and orchestrating specialized agents for robust industrial decision-making.

我的研究关注如何让工业智能从单点算法走向具备感知、推理、协作与行动能力的系统， 面向真实生产环境中的数据异构、任务复杂和场景迁移问题。

CORE QUESTION

How can machines learn shared industrial knowledge—and collaborate to solve tasks beyond a single model?
